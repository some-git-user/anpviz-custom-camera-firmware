There is an example to forward your video stream to YouTube-Live, do the following steps:
1. The premise is that you registered at YouTube to use the live service.
2. Live streaming has to be activated in your YouTube account (Help).
3. The URL to your live dashboard on YouTube is: https://www.youtube.com/live_dashboard
4. Find out your RTMP-URL and the uniquie streaming ID.
rtmp://a.rtmp.youtube.com/live2/[your_channe/ID] will look like: rtmp://a.rtmp.youtube.com/live2/yourkey-1234.1yyz-4g41-1aq4-abcd
5. Enable and save

以上参数的含义是什么？例如RTMP推流URL如下，以字符 / 拆分可以得到对应的参数。
rtmp://xxx.xxx.xxx/live/4287d69ef405428c?wsSecret=5ba27f7bb727f1b8249d0e1404a398a8&wsTime=5acd71a
1) 
服务器地址
xxx.xxx.xxx
2) 
端口号
缺省时默认是1935，如果xxx.xxx.xxx后面接着 '：数字'，该数字表示端口号
3) 
应用名称
live
4) 
流ID
4287d69ef405428c?wsSecret=5ba27f7bb727f1b8249d0e1404a398a8&wsTime=5acd71a
